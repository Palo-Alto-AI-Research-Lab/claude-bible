# Declined decisions — the journal of what we decided NOT to do

> Memory stores what is true NOW. This journal stores what we ruled OUT and why — the half plain memory lacks. Without it, a rejected idea silently resurfaces weeks later as if fresh.

**How to use:**
- **Before proposing** anything structural (a new layer, tool swap, restructure, automation) — scan this log. If it's here as rejected/deferred, don't re-pitch unless a `Revisit if` condition now holds, or reopen it EXPLICITLY as a trade-off quoting the original decline.
- **When the owner rejects or defers** something non-trivial — append an entry the SAME session. Capture at the moment, not weeks later.
- **When a verdict is OVERTURNED** (see objection sparring in SPEC §6) — don't delete the entry. Mark it `⚡ OVERTURNED <date>` at the top with a pointer to the new decision, and keep the original text as history. Parallel sessions must never cite a dead verdict as live.

**Entry format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>
- Proposed: <what was suggested>
- Decision: rejected | deferred | superseded
- Why: <the owner's reason, grounded in their words where possible>
- Revisit if: <condition that reopens it, or "—">
```
